## 1. Failure localization this experiment attacks (from the L3-3 record)

(ii) stage-2 pair factoring never adopted (no APRules → hypothetical

## 2. Candidate E, fully specified

Everything Candidate D had (evidence tracking; genesis at MDL-cheapest
spelling; generic condition/member repair diagnosis with citations —
unchanged (d) machinery; re-entrant generic compression over every
production with decoys generated and scored each pass; attribution
inference) with these localized changes:

1. **Two-tier identification.** Tier 1 (primary): canonical-signature
   classing — the triple (tap@{pristine,charred}, heat@pristine,
   soak@{pristine,charred}) keys class identity; a completed signature maps
   to its class or, if novel, pools for genesis. Signatures are unambiguous
   in any deterministic world of this interface and eliminate D's
   duplicate-class churn (no merge machinery needed or included). Tier 2
   (fallback, for objects whose signature cannot complete — weathered
   arrivals, partially observed objects): rank classes by (conflicting
   cells, −positive matches) against current rules; assert to the unique
   minimum with ≥ 2 positive matches, ≤ 1 conflict, and a ≥ 1-conflict gap
   to the runner-up. The ≤ 1-conflict allowance is deliberate: a best-fit
   assertion that turns out wrong produces the violation that feeds the
   (d) repair streams (13.3 bits risked against 3.46/query abstention —
   MDL-rational), and it is how cracked objects become identifiable before
   any CR rule exists.
2. **Targeted pair exploration.** Exploration priority: (a) complete
   signatures of unidentified objects; (b) grind pairs whose class-pair
   cell is unknown to the library (the stage-2 evidence D never
   accumulated); (c) probe unobserved unary cells of identified members;
   (d) uniform (seeded). Grind selection is evidence-directed, not random.
3. **Stage-2 unblocked by construction:** with tier-1 classes clean and
   pair evidence targeted, the dense-core matrix and the pair-region search
   operate on essentially exact tables; adoption remains a pure measured-
   compression competition (unchanged arithmetic, decoys included).

Learned/authority split (invariant b): Candidate E, like D, holds no
authority — every prediction, admission, and bookkeeping action is the
substrate's; E only proposes. A12 both directions: the move inventory spans
every production including all decoys (P7, P9, P10, ord-modes), generated
and scored by the same exact-compression arithmetic each pass;
generated/scored/adopted counts logged per run.

## AMENDMENT 1 (2026-08-29, development-stage, committed BEFORE the digest
## freeze and before any scored run — invariant (c) intact)

Development on streams 997–999 (only) falsified several parts of the §2/§3
design as written. Every change below is a contender-side change: harness,
world, criteria text, thresholds, audits, and certification machinery are
untouched at their frozen digests. The verdict streams (23, 29, 31) have
had no contender exposure. The final design is what the freeze digest
covers; §2/§3 as originally written are superseded by this amendment and
retained above unedited (invariant i).

### A1.1 What development falsified, with the dev evidence

1. **Tier-2 as specified never fires.** The gap-1 requirement is
   structurally unsatisfiable for cracked objects: before any CR rule
   exists, the true class and a tap-swap partner tie at (1 conflict,
   2 positives) on every cracked profile — and one cracked kind's profile
   is IDENTICAL to another kind's pristine profile (the crack edit
   collapses tap to two symbols keyed on the first attribute), so the
   impostor outranks the truth at (0, 3). Measured: weathered accuracy
   0.000 on all dev streams, 0 of 298 cracked object-episodes ever
   identified; with naive tie-breaks, 50%-wrong cohort locks and 82–138
   junk classes from repair churn.
2. **Tier-2 racing tier-1 on pristine objects** mis-asserts on 2-cell
   evidence and split-churns junk classes (105 splits, 93 size-1 on dev
   998).
3. **Stage-1-then-stage-2 adoption never reaches the pair layer** (the
   Candidate D failure repeating): every true-partition attribute set
   scores NEGATIVE under the incremental arithmetic — flat rules are sunk
   costs, retire fees are real, and the one positive plan was a
   free-riding attribute whose aurules carried no literals (all-constant
   columns). Measured: best 3-set gain −168 to −547 across dev streams;
   the free-rider certified nothing (no referencing rules, no composite).
4. **Early adoption is path-dependent poison**: adopting off the earliest
   settled columns (ep 24, 4-class core — the rare kind matures late)
   locks partitions the discriminative columns cannot use; heat@pristine
   and tap@pristine never receive attr rules, the k_new class stays
   unattributable (inference ambiguous between two assignments), and the
   attribute units' ablation margins stay at 0.01–0.03.

### A1.2 The design as frozen (supersedes §2 items 1–3)

**Identification.**
- Tier 1 unchanged (canonical signature; signature-keyed genesis; no-op
  merge).
- Tier 2 applies ONLY to cracked-condition objects (cracked is absorbing;
  everything else completes tier 1), and only with the full 3-action
  cracked profile. A completed cracked profile is itself a deterministic
  signature ("crsig"); each crsig resolves ONCE to a class and the mapping
  is cohort-permanent (`crsig_map`), remapped only by the split machinery.
- Cohort resolution evidence, in strict priority: (i) grind
  discrimination — outcomes of grinds of the unidentified object against
  identified members, compared with the settled class-pair evidence table;
  while an informative partner (one whose settled pair entries differ
  across candidates) is present and unused, resolution defers and
  exploration grinds; (ii) learned edit-pattern ("organ") statistics —
  per-action [stable, changed] counts accumulated ONLY from
  grind-verified resolutions; once the pattern is established (some organ
  proven stable, some proven edited), candidates are scored by matching
  cracked outcomes against pristine cells weighted by organ stability,
  and cracked-cell class evidence weighted toward the EDITED organ (the
  only organ whose CR cells carry identity); (iii) exact-condition cell
  evidence. The winner must be strict on the (discrimination, stability,
  cell-evidence) triple; ties defer. Candidate filter: conflicts <= 1,
  positives >= 2 under `_consistency_ev` (rule prediction where the
  library resolves; class cell majorities where it does not; for cracked
  cells, explicit CR rules first, then the learned pattern, raw
  majorities only during bootstrap).
- Rationale: identification authority never leaves the substrate; all of
  this is proposal-side evidence weighing. Wrong resolutions remain
  possible and are repaired by the unchanged (d) machinery (which also
  remaps the cohort on splits).

**Exploration** (supersedes §2 item 2): (a) complete signatures;
unidentified cracked objects are probed to a full profile; (a2) grind an
unmapped cracked object against the most informative identified partner;
(b) grind class-pairs unknown to the library; (c) build class-matrix
support — probe cells of identified members wherever class evidence is
below the matrix support threshold, deliberately charring a pristine
member when its class lacks charred-column support; (d) uniform (seeded).

**Evidence-narrow repair pass** (added): any rule whose condition mask
includes a condition where the class's own settled cell evidence
contradicts the rule's outcome is revised to exclude it; one batch per
class (locality); violation citations attached. This dissolves the
overlapping-mask deadlock at never-observed conditions once evidence
lands.

**Adoption — joint, normal-form, full-swap** (supersedes §2 item 3 and
§3's "unchanged arithmetic" for the adoption CRITERION only):
- Candidates and aurule-plan construction are unchanged (dense core,
  outcome-group partitions, block-constancy, <= 2 literals, decoys
  generated and scored every pass by the same criterion).
- Selection is a bounded exhaustive subset search (all subsets of size
  <= 4 of the top-12 single-partition candidates) instead of greedy.
- The criterion is a NORMAL-FORM comparison: the plan's cost (P4s,
  attributions, P5s, P6s) against the FLAT spelling of the same settled
  evidence it covers — two normal forms of today's knowledge — rather
  than against whichever rules the incremental history holds. The
  incremental form is the measured hill-climbing trap of A1.1(3). Decoys
  are priced by the SAME normal-form criterion and still fail (each covers
  no evidence more cheaply than the flat form; adopted decoys across all
  dev runs: 0 of 1057–1290 generated per run).
- Attribute and pair layers are scored and emitted as ONE plan (the pair
  savings legitimately subsidize the attribute layer; both are exact).
- Emission is a full swap: rules fully inside the covered conditions are
  retired; straddling rules are retired and their uncovered residual
  evidence respelled as fresh P2s (retire+new, never revise — the (d)
  stream stays purely the repair machinery's); overflow batches queue
  across boundaries.
- Timing gates: no first adoption until every pristine column is settled
  for the whole core AND the core carries five classes (fallback ep 150).
- The learned/authority split is intact: admission remains the
  substrate's legality+budget check; all scoring above is proposal-side.
